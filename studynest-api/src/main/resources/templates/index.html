Study Center Guidelines

1. Arrival & Check-In Grace Window

When a seat is reserved, it appears in Yellow. Students must confirm their check-in within 15 minutes of the start time to turn their seat to Red (Occupied).

2. Cancellation Policy

To protect other students, only the student who reserved the seat can cancel their booking. Other students cannot cancel your seat. Center administrators hold overriding authority.

3. Zones & Facilities

Seats 1 to 6 are in the Quiet Study Zone. Seats 7 to 14 are in the Central Workstation area. Seats 15 to 20 are situated by the Window Study Bay.
